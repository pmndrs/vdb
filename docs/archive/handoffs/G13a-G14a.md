## Notes

- The physical refraction samples its own `viewportMipTexture` (three's back-side instance is module-private): one more
  framebuffer copy with mips per render while a glass level set draws. Not timed.
- Glass behind glass: three's transmission samples the frame as it was when the first transmissive object drew, so a
  glass piece does not see another through itself, and glass casts an opaque shadow (G13c).
- The physical probe needs a light in the scene for transmission to run at all (three skips lighting without one); the
  harness adds `AmbientLight(…, 0)` (Trap).
- The shadow e2e is the first gate on the material's orthographic ray path (written in K3.7, ungated until now).
- Surface nets: not done (K3.7c); the trace covers display.
- Demo 14 does not stream `.wvdb` points yet: `vdbToWvdb` bakes no point grids (G14b).
