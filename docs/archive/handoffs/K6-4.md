Design: [K6-core-renderer.md](../../dev/design/K6-core-renderer.md) §3.3–§3.4 (spectral: pipeline key, stacked targets,
alpha prefix), D34. Builds on [K6-2.md](./K6-2.md), [K6-2b.md](./K6-2b.md), [K6-3.md](./K6-3.md), [K6-5.md](./K6-5.md),

**Landed:** `settings.spectral` in the core for both lightings: a `spectral` pipeline key on every kernel with a spectral
variant (gather 'volume' and 'frame', shadow grid, transmittance, environment grid, resolve, composite, volume shadow
maps), CPU twins, probes with fail-before mutations, and `./three` `backend: 'core'` with `spectral: true` (the core's
"spectral is K6.4" warning is gone). The emission light (probes) stays grey, as his `FrameEmissionLight` (no spectral
branch); volume emission inside the spectral gathers works per channel.

## Design changes (please review)

1. **One transmittance dispatch for every slot group** (his one pass per group; the design said one dispatch per group):
   the group is gid.z / (R · count), so no per-dispatch uniform and one pipeline.
2. **The shadow grid is two textures with one layout** (design: "2 stacks"), so the absorption shares the extinction's
   origins; partials are 3 words per voxel.
3. **The spectral history is one texture twice as deep** (scatter and alpha halves) so the resolve stays at 3 storage
   textures (the default limit is 4).
4. **Spectral volume shadow maps are two textures** (grey map + `rgbTexture`), not extra layers (the design's "+ spectral
   layers"): a cube view takes exactly 6 layers.
5. **The alpha prefix** is rg16float with `texture-formats-tier1`, else rgba16float (written directly; the K7.3
   buffer-and-copy trick is not needed: the resolve writes one texel per froxel either way).
6. **`render()` with spectral needs `targets.sceneColor`** and draws without blending.

## DIVERGES rows (provisional, from V130; source mjurczyk 75e7345 unless noted)

- V130 — K6.4: the spectral transmittance runs every slot group in one dispatch (group = gid.z / (R · cascades)), the 3
  targets of each group and cascade are sub-volumes of the one transmittance stack (`transmittanceOrigin[3 (g · count + c) + k]`),
  and the stack keeps the most groups used (his one pass per group into 3 MRT targets per group; `renderFrameLighting`,
  `getSpectralTransmittanceTarget`). Same values (twin: every group 0 over).
- V131 — K6.4: the spectral shadow grid is two stacked 3D textures with one layout (extinction rgb, absorption rgb); his
  two MRT targets of the grid atlas (`FrameShadowDensityMaterial` spectral branch). Multi-caster partials hold 3 words per
  voxel. Same values.
- V132 — K6.4: spectral froxels are two 3D textures, `scatter` (L·α rgb, α green) and `scatterAlpha` (α red, α blue, 0,
  α green) (his two atlas targets). Overlapping volumes combine per channel: 'mixture' sums τ and τL of each channel,
  'over' blends each channel with its own α; his hardware blend used the green α for both targets
  (`FrameInjectMaterial`, `FrameLightingMaterial` spectral outputs). One volume per froxel: identical.
- V133 — K6.4: the spectral resolve keeps T of each channel in the column scan (stop when every T < 0.001, his
  maxComponent break) and writes a second prefix (red, blue opacity; rg16float with `texture-formats-tier1`, else
  rgba16float); the history stacks both textures along z (his two history atlas targets, one reprojection). The composite
  applies the two-tap formula per channel and blends over the scene colour itself, no hardware blending (his
  `createSpectralFragmentNode`, NoBlending); `render()` then needs `targets.sceneColor`.
- V134 — K6.4: spectral `'mlp'` / `'neural'` and `'multi-scale'` run as 1D diffusion (his `isNeural` for 'neural'; K7.3's
  multi-scale lookup is grey). Two-sided, octaves and diffusion run per channel (his).
- V135 — K6.4: spectral volume shadow maps are two textures: the map with (first, last) × the channel mean and its twin
  `rgbTexture` with the optical depth of each channel (his second render target; a cube view needs exactly 6 layers, so
  not extra layers). Receivers `vdbVolumeShadowRgb` / `vdbVolumeShadowCubeRgb`; a ShadowMaterial gets the channel mean.
- V136 — K6.4 × K6.5b: host occluder maps in spectral 'frame' lighting: the transmittance occludes each channel's
  scattered part per voxel (as grey), the lighting part multiplies each slot's direct part (octaves: the whole light) by the
  visibility at the summed visible position (grey: per volume at its visible position; his spectral density pass has no
  slots). Not in his renderer.
