## 3. Multi-scale with the paper's σ = 2^k levels

**What it is.** His binomial chain blurs level k with σ_k² = (4^k − 1)/4 base voxels² (σ ≈ 2^(k−1)): the 1 3 3 1
filter has variance ¾ per halving. The paper (Disney "Deep Scattering" §4.2) blurs level k with σ = 2^k, the level's
own stencil spacing. Level k + 1 of the same chain has σ_{k+1} ≈ 2^k, so `multiScaleSigma: 'paper'` makes lookup level
k read mip k + 1 (same tap positions and segment); the chain holds one more level (at most 8; past it the last level
reads the last mip). No new pass or binding: the `VdbFrameMultiScale` uniform's padding word became `levelOffset`
(0 = unchanged, so `'his'` computes what it did; only the multi-scale transmittance's WGSL grew: 15,286 → 15,355 B min).

The his-σ, diffusion, two-sided, octaves and neural rows reproduce K6.3's table to 0.1 points (references re-rendered
at the same queries). σ = 2^k moves the energy by +0.5 to +2.9 points on the thick media (more light leaks through
the wider bulk near edges), 0 at albedo 0.5, and makes the thin bunny and the point light worse (−5.3, −1.1). Over
the 6 sun / point scenes, mean |bias| / mean median error: diffusion 50.2 / 45.8 %, multi-scale his σ 48.2 / 44.7 %,
paper σ 48.4 / 44.8 %, octaves 46.0 / 52.4 %, two-sided 22.1 / 26.2 %, neural 14.9 / 21.7 %.

**Why multi-scale stays at diffusion.** The lookup only changes the optical depth fed to the same transfer function as
1D diffusion: scattered = exp(−D(bulk)) − exp(−bulk), D the 1D diffusion depth min(d, d_a + ln(1 + ¾ d_s)) of the
blurred bulk. A blur preserves the mass along the light path inside the medium, so the bulk ≈ the marched depth and
the result ≈ diffusion; a wider σ only moves light near edges and in thin parts (−5.3 to +2.9 points of bias). At
albedo 0.5, D picks the direct depth for every depth below ~16 (d_a ≈ 0.95 d), so multi-scale (either σ) is single
scattering there, as diffusion is (K6.R1 finding 1). Neither σ touches that.

**Recommendation: drop `'multi-scale'` as a default candidate** (keep it as his opt-in mode for parity, both σ behind
the setting until K6.8 decides the API; or delete `multiScaleSigma` with the loser). It is the weakest MS family next
to diffusion in every scene, its edge mode and σ move it by < 1 point, and it cannot fix the albedo-0.5 failure,
because the transfer function, not the bulk estimate, is the limit. The remaining candidates for the default are
neural (best median error in most scenes, best energy with ambient occlusion on the absorbing bunny) and two-sided
(best energy on his bunny and thin media). If multi-scale is to be rescued, the step is the paper's real method (the
stencil into a network), not the bulk's σ.
